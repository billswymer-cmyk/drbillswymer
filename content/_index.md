Dr. Bill Swymer is an Teaching Assistant Professor of Real Estate in the Department of Economics, Finance and Legal Studies at the University of Alabama's Culverhouse College of Business. He holds a Ph.D. in Real Estate from the University of Georgia. He is a licensed real estate broker in Alabama, Georgia and Massachusetts. His research has appeared in the *Journal of Housing Economics*.
