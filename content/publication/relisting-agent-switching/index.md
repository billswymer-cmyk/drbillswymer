title: "Relisting, Agent Switching, and Sale Outcomes in the Housing Market"

publication: "*Journal of Housing Economics*, Vol. 69, Article 102086"
abstract: "Using more than 670,000 home sales between 2000 and 2019, including nearly 45,000 relistings, this paper examines how relisting a home — with the same agent or a different one — affects sale price and total marketing duration. Outcomes depend on the phase of the housing cycle, the length of the off-market gap between listings, and whether the original listing expired or was canceled. Relistings during the 2005–2006 market peak show positive price effects, while relistings in other periods tend to sell at a discount; short off-market gaps (0–7 days) are associated with longer total marketing periods, consistent with buyers viewing a quick re-listing with skepticism."
