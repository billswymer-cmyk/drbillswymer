Bill Swymer is an Assistant Teaching Professor of Real Estate and Finance in the Department of Economics, Finance and Legal Studies at the University of Alabama's Culverhouse College of Business.

His path to academia ran through industry: he earned a B.S. in Business Administration from Endicott College in 2005 and an M.S. in Finance from Bentley University in 2012 before returning to school for a Ph.D. in Real Estate from the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business, completed in 2024.

His research examines agent and seller behavior in residential real estate markets — in particular, how the decision to relist a home, and whether to switch listing agents when doing so, affects the eventual sale price and time on market. His paper "Relisting, Agent Switching, and Sale Outcomes in the Housing Market," co-authored with Darren K. Hayunga (University of Georgia), was published in the *Journal of Housing Economics* in 2025. Drawing on more than 670,000 home sales, the study finds that the effect of relisting on outcomes depends on the phase of the housing cycle, the length of time a home sits off-market between listings, and whether the original listing expired or was canceled.

At Culverhouse, he teaches courses in real estate and finance to undergraduate and graduate students.
